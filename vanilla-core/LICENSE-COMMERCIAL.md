Vanilla Core is licensed by default under the GNU Affero General Public
License v3.0 (`LICENSE`). AGPL-3.0 is free to use, modify, and redistribute,
with one condition: if you distribute a modified version, or run it as a
network service others interact with, you must make your complete
corresponding source available under AGPL-3.0 too. That condition is the
mechanism behind "free to use, and what you build with it stays free."

If that copyleft condition doesn't work for you — most commonly, you want to
ship a closed-source product built on Vanilla Core, or embed it in something
you're not willing to open-source — a commercial license is intended to be
available as an alternative that waives the copyleft (share-alike)
requirement in exchange for a fee.

**This document is a statement of intent, not a signed contract.** The
actual commercial terms (price, scope, support commitments, term length,
liability caps) have not been drafted yet and need an attorney's input
before they're offered to anyone. As a rough, adjustable guideline: this is
aimed at organizations with meaningful revenue (a placeholder figure like
USD $1,000,000/year gross revenue is a reasonable starting point for "when
to expect someone to reach out"), not at individuals, students, indie
developers, nonprofits, or small commercial users — all of whom are already
well served by the free AGPL-3.0 path and are not the target of this option.

To ask about a commercial license, contact the maintainer directly. Until
real terms exist, treat this file as "ask, don't assume."
